    If you've already mixed chemicals

    1. Leave the room now. Take children and pets. Don't stay to clean up.
    2. Get fresh air. Go outside or to a room with open windows. Only open windows on your way out if you can do it without breathing the fumes.
    3. Don't add anything else to "neutralise" it. That can make more gas.
    4. Rinse skin or eyes with clean running water for 15 minutes. Take off splashed clothes.
    5. Get help if you have coughing, wheezing, chest tightness, trouble breathing, or burning eyes. Keep the bottles so you can read the labels to the doctor.

    This is general first-aid guidance, not medical advice. If someone is struggling to breathe or unconscious, call emergency services first.
